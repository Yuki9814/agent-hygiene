# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors, maintainers, and users must make participation respectful,
welcoming, and free from harassment. Discuss behavior and evidence rather than
attacking people. Respect privacy, security embargoes, and requests to remove
sensitive material.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
intimidation, sexualized attention, publishing another person's private
information, or knowingly submitting live credentials or confidential content.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the repository's
security advisory channel. The maintainer may edit or remove content, close
interactions, restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when
necessary to protect the community.

Reports will be reviewed as privately as practical. Conflicts involving the
current maintainer should be escalated through GitHub's abuse-reporting tools.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is officially
representing the project elsewhere.
